# ToolHearth — Owner Actions

Everything not listed here is part of the site build.

## Required to collect revenue

- [ ] Apply to the affiliate programs you want to promote.
- [ ] Complete each program's identity, tax, and payout setup.
- [ ] Send the approved affiliate destination URLs for web hosting, email marketing, CRM, AI writing, AI marketing, and VPN services.
- [ ] Choose a checkout provider (Stripe Payment Links, Gumroad, or Lemon Squeezy).
- [ ] Approve the first products, prices, refund terms, and license terms.
- [ ] Send the final checkout URL for each approved product.

## Required for email ownership

- [ ] In Vercel, confirm `RESEND_API_KEY`, `RESEND_AUDIENCE_ID`, `CONTACT_TO_EMAIL`, and `CONTACT_FROM_EMAIL`.
- [ ] Verify the sending domain inside Resend.
- [ ] Send one real test subscription and one contact form submission after deployment.

## Required for search and advertising accounts

- [ ] Verify toolhearth.com in Google Search Console and Bing Webmaster Tools.
- [ ] Submit `https://toolhearth.com/sitemap.xml` in both.
- [ ] Connect Google Analytics or another analytics account if you want reporting beyond Vercel Analytics.
- [ ] Apply to an ad network only when the site meets that network's traffic and content requirements.
- [ ] Send the approved publisher and ad-slot IDs; ad containers remain hidden until then.

## Editorial approvals

- [ ] Confirm that every product named in a comparison page was actually reviewed or tested.
- [ ] Approve rankings, claims, screenshots, and pricing before affiliate buttons go live.
- [ ] Review revenue, conversion, and traffic data monthly and decide which pages deserve expansion.

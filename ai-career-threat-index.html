      How the score works

      Each of the 300 US occupations in this index is scored 0–100 on overall AI displacement risk, then broken into two sub-scores:

      • Task Automation — how much of the role’s routine work existing AI already completes (resets, data entry, transcription, routing, drafting).
      • Agentic Exposure — how much of the role is an end-to-end pipeline an AI agent can own without human handoff.

      Roles scoring 75+ face severe near-term pressure; 50–74 are being restructured; under 35 remain largely human-led. The score is a research estimate for career planning, not a prediction for any specific employer.
